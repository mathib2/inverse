# Algorithms with a paper behind them, and where they live

Each entry: what the method is, the reference, where it runs in this code, and what it changes. Numbers are measured, not claimed (`docs/evidence/EVAL.md`).

| Method | Reference | Where | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structural identifiability before fitting** (rank of the sigma-whitened Jacobian in log-parameter space, Fisher spectrum) | Rothenberg 1971 (identification); Raue et al. 2009 (practical identifiability by profile/Fisher analysis); Gutenkunst et al. 2007 (sloppy models) | `inference/identifiability.py`, `analysis.identify` | The agent refuses before any fit when only parameter ratios are determined, and names the unresolved direction. 20/20 planted cases refused. |
| **D-optimal experimental design** (maximise det F over candidate experiments) | Fedorov 1972; Pukelsheim 1993 | `inference/next_measurement.py` | The refusal comes with the measurement that would resolve it (a forced response with a known force), ranked by rank then log det F. |
| **MAP fit with Laplace uncertainty and Birge scaling** | Laplace approximation; Birge 1932 (scaling by reduced chi-square) | `inference/fit.py` | Intervals widen when the sensor spec understates the noise. Measured 95% coverage: 93% (c), 100% (k). |
| **Split conformal prediction band** for the held-out rollout | Vovk, Gammerman and Shafer 2005; Lei et al. 2018; Angelopoulos and Bates 2021 | `inference/conformal.py`, `analysis.finalize` | Fit on the first 50% of the record, calibrate the band on the next 20% (never fitted), test on the unseen last 30%. Distribution-free: under exchangeability the band covers ≥90% in expectation; the measured coverage is reported next to the nominal level because time-series residuals are not exactly exchangeable. |
| **Information criteria + held-out rollout** | Akaike 1974; Schwarz 1978 | `inference/selection.py` | A better in-sample fit does not win by itself; the held-out tail and the physics validators decide. |
| **Closed-form solution of the linear oscillator** | elementary (characteristic roots) | `models.linear_free_decay`, used by `simulate` for the linear unforced case | Exact instead of LSODA: no integration error, no tolerance dependence, and 341x faster per evaluation on the workstation (0.04 ms vs 15.3 ms for 400 samples); a full P-104 analysis went from about 4.8 s to 2.7 s, the rest being the nonlinear candidate, which still integrates. The 102-test suite runs in 58 s instead of 110 s. |
| **Sparse regression as a proposer** (PySINDy, no sparsification) | Brunton, Proctor and Kutz 2016 | `inference/sindy.py`, `adapters/physical.candidate_library` | The most flexible model always faces the validators; the trap seed shows it fitting better and being vetoed. |
| **Reflexion: verbal lessons kept across attempts** | Shinn et al. 2023 | `team/memory.py: add_lesson / lessons`, `team/lead.py` (written on a reviewer's send-back, injected into the expert's next tasks), `team/experts.py: BaseExpert._task_text` | An expert sees what reviewers told it before (H-MEM key `team:lessons:<expert>`), so a corrected mistake is not repeated on the next task. |
| **Self-consistency for planning** | Wang et al. 2022 | `team/lead.py: Lead._vote` (`scripts/run_team.py --votes N`) | Several plans are sampled at temperature 0.7 and the most common task/expert structure is kept; the vote is traced (`plan_votes`). Opt-in: it costs N planning calls. |
| **Plan, execute, verify, replan** | the orchestrator literature; the team's own spec | `team/lead.py` (Critic accept/revise/reject with retries, stuck detector, pending experts created by the architect, verbatim failure reporting) | Nothing reaches the answer without review; failures are reported by code, not paraphrased by the model. |

Not used, and why: residual bootstrap or jackknife+ for parameter intervals (Efron 1979; Barber et al. 2021) would need dozens of refits per analysis; the measured Laplace coverage is close to nominal, so it stays future work. Tree-of-thoughts style search for the Reasoning expert costs several model calls per step for a demo that already runs minutes.
